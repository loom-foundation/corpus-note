The standing register of every verb in force in this corpus.
It is the record the uniqueness law is checked against: corpus-wide, verb names and their computed inverse names draw from one pool, and no two entries in that pool collide.
A verb is registered here before its declaration is authored; the source of every signature pair is the authoring end, the inverse computed.

| Verb | Signature | Inverse | Slice traversal | Constraints | Declaration |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| addresses | requirement → need | addressedBy | yes | – | declared (draft) |
| qualifies | acceptance-criterion → requirement | qualifiedBy | yes | – | undeclared |
| decidedBy | requirement → decision | decides | yes | – | undeclared |
| upholds | convention → principle; decision → principle | upheldBy | yes | – | undeclared |
