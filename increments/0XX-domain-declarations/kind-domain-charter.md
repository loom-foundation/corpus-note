The meta-shape of a domain charter, fixed here as an ordinary specification until floor 1's own meta-kind exists.
A charter's frontmatter carries the domain's lowercase token as `domain`; the charter is the domain's one home.

A domain charter carries, in order:

1. **The coverage lead**: the prose intension of the domain; the disciplines it owns live in this lead, never in a separate section.
2. **The inclusion test**: an operational membership predicate, answerable yes or no for a concrete artefact.
3. **Boundaries**: which neighbouring statements belong to sibling domains; a boundary is mutual, and a hand-off is claimed by the receiver.
4. **Origin**, marked informative: the frameworks the domain draws discipline from; provenance, never a normative reference, and adopting an authority's obligations remains a `draws:` of its corpus.

A charter carries no process prose, no maturity or roadmap material, and no restated kind definitions; a kind's definition lives in its kind declaration.
